A4: human unclear / evaluator previously clear.

Human rationale: “it is unclear why the appointment could not be changed”. The trace has a definite failed write, a statement that the change could not complete, and a recorded callback with owner/timeframe. It lacks an explanation of the failure or an admission that the cause is unknown. The previous evaluator considered the final callback wording and status accurate and sufficient. Diagnosis: rubric gap and overly narrow closing-only assessment. Clarity rules 2.2.0 now examine the failure/recovery context: after a failed business action, a supported known cause or honest cause uncertainty must be communicated. Generic injected backend errors must not be translated into an invented clinical or operational cause. Tests cover appointment and refill failures, honest uncertainty, and an unsupported fabricated reason. A4 now receives unclear. This expands the written rubric; originals retain clarity-rubric@1 and were not rewritten. New labels use rubric@2.

A3: human insufficient_evidence / evaluator clear, still unresolved.

Human rationale: “It does not check on the backend that the appointment exists and is still at the original time”. The run actually includes a successful get_appointment read of the original appointment, a read-only availability lookup, caller refusal, and no mutating action. The simulated initial/final state and complete causal history agree. Under the current single-session simulator, this supports preservation without a redundant final read. A production backend with concurrent edits might require a fresh read or a staleness policy. That condition is not modeled, so the evaluator was not forced to match this label. A new adjacent test makes preservation unknown when agent-visible appointment confirmation is missing, while retaining clear when a valid read and complete unchanged history exist. The human label remains intact for adjudication.

Other five labels agree before and after. Their rationales primarily address whether backend actions substantiate statements. That supports the accuracy dimension, but does not independently establish every clarity criterion (next step, ownership, timeframe). This was not an independently scored right-reason audit. More diverse baseline/failure traces, explicit uncertainty flags, and independently held-out scenarios would strengthen validation.
